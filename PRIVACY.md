# Privacy Policy — ExitMOCK

**ExitMOCK by YomTech**  
Version 1.1.0 · Effective date: 2026-09-02

## Summary

ExitMOCK is a mock exit-exam app for GiT DTU Electromechanical Engineering students. **Exam questions and AI tip models run on your device.** The app does **not** require an account and does **not** include ads.

## Data on your device

Stored only on your phone (not on ExitMOCK / YomTech servers):

- App settings (theme, exam options, default tips mode)
- Optional single best mock result (full Q&A) when “Save my Best” is on
- Question bank and on-device AI models bundled in the app

## Network & third parties

- **Optional network AI tips:** Some builds include a recommender URL. When set, Results may send your mock attempt (score, answers, questions, timing) to that HTTPS endpoint. The developer hosts this server; it is not a third-party ad network. If unreachable, tips use on-device models.
- **Share / export:** PDF export uses the system share sheet or Downloads folder. We do not receive those files.
- **Crash reports (optional):** Production builds may include Sentry when configured; only crash diagnostics, no exam answers unless you enable custom logging later.

## Permissions (Android)

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| Storage (API ≤28 only) | Saving PDF exports on older Android; API 29+ uses MediaStore Downloads |

The app does **not** request camera, microphone, location, or contacts.

## Data we collect on our servers

We do **not** run an account system and do **not** collect, store on our servers, or sell personal data from normal app use.

## Children

The app is for university exam preparation. It does not target children under 13 and does not knowingly collect children’s personal data.
